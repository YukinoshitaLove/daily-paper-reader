## 摘要
深度展开方法在编码孔径快照光谱成像（CASSI）重建中取得了最先进的性能，但通常依赖于假设理想光学系统的闭式数据保真更新。在实际CASSI系统中，依赖视场和波长的点扩散函数（PSF）引入了卷积耦合，打破了正规方程的对角结构，使得闭式更新无法适用。我们提出了一个深度展开框架，通过三项贡献来解决这一根本性的算法挑战：（1）K步共轭梯度展开，在包含PSF的前向算子下显式求解非对角正规方程；（2）一个带有波长自适应步长的可学习梯度细化模块，步长由每个波长的PSF嵌入生成；（3）一个PSF条件化的惩罚估计器，根据光学退化程度调整ADMM正则化强度。蒙特卡洛PSF训练策略进一步提高了对制造引起的PSF变化的鲁棒性。我们的方法在KAIST数据集上达到30.53 dB，比使用相近参数量（1.42M）的DPU基线（1.27M）提高了2.73 dB，并且比使用33%更少参数量的更大基线模型（DPU-B+，2.12M）提高了1.70 dB。

## Abstract
Deep unfolding methods achieve state-of-the-art performance in coded aperture snapshot spectral imaging (CASSI) reconstruction but typically rely on closed-form data-fidelity updates that assume an ideal optical system. In practical CASSI systems, the field-dependent and wavelength-dependent point spread function (PSF) introduces convolutional coupling that breaks the diagonal structure of the normal equation, rendering closed-form updates inapplicable. We propose a deep unfolding framework that addresses this fundamental algorithmic challenge through three contributions: (1)~a $K$-step conjugate gradient (CG) unrolling that explicitly solves the non-diagonal normal equation under PSF-inclusive forward operators; (2)~a learned gradient refinement module with wavelength-adaptive step sizes generated from a per-wavelength PSF embedding; and (3)~a PSF-conditioned penalty estimator that adapts the ADMM regularization strength to the optical degradation severity. A Monte Carlo PSF training strategy further improves robustness to manufacturing-induced PSF variations. Our method achieves 30.53~dB on the KAIST dataset, +2.73~dB over the DPU baseline (1.27M) using a comparable number of parameters (1.42M), and +1.70~dB over a larger baseline (DPU-B+, 2.12M) using 33\% fewer parameters.
