## 摘要
生成模型支持跨文本、图像、视频和设计领域的内容创作，但其接口主要依赖自然语言。尽管自然语言在指定高层意图方面有效，但对于细粒度控制、迭代细化和开放式探索而言，可能低效且不精确。我们提出 PolyGandr，一个用于探索潜在表示与生成输出之间关系的可视化界面，作为自然语言模型引导的补充。PolyGandr 将生成控制表述为高维导航，使用户能够通过统一的“地图与混合调色板”界面定义起点、引导方向和步长。将全局探索与局部细化相结合，既支持广泛的设计空间导航，也支持细粒度的输出操控，而无需反复将视觉意图转化为提示词。我们在三个用例中评估 PolyGandr：StyleGAN2 人脸生成、Stable Diffusion 椅子设计和旋转剪纸工程。一项形成性研究表明，PolyGandr 通过支持实时编辑，同时提供概览和探索更广泛设计空间的手段，补充了现有工作流。

## Abstract
Generative models support content creation across text, images, videos, and design disciplines, yet their interfaces rely primarily on natural language. While effective for specifying high-level intent, language can be inefficient and imprecise for fine-grained control, iterative refinement, and open-ended exploration. We present PolyGandr, a visual interface for exploring relationships between latent representations and generated outputs, complementing natural-language model steering. PolyGandr formulates generative control as high-dimensional navigation, enabling users to define starting points, steering directions, and step sizes through a unified map-and-mixing-palette interface. Combining global exploration with local refinement supports both broad design-space navigation and fine-grained output manipulation without repeatedly translating visual intent into prompts. We evaluate PolyGandr across three use cases: StyleGAN2 face generation, Stable Diffusion chair design, and rotational kirigami engineering. A formative study indicates that PolyGandr complements existing workflows by supporting real-time editing while simultaneously providing an overview and means to explore the broader design space.
