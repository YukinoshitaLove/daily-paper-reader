## 摘要
多变量无标度纹理图像如今在许多性质迥异的现代应用中处于核心地位。一个常见的挑战性问题是，能够从（可能小得多的）小规模学习集生成任意尺寸的此类多变量纹理。参数模型对于生成真实纹理往往过于受限，因此促使采用基于神经网络训练的生成方法。尽管若干神经网络方法提出生成比训练尺寸更大的纹理，但它们并未通过量化纹理中多尺度或无标度统计量的复现程度来明确评估生成质量，而更多依赖感知评估。本工作将卷积 Wasserstein-GAN 架构与自注意力层相结合，以生成任意尺寸的纹理，同时专注于复现多尺度统计量，而无需将模型编码到损失函数中。研究了使用自注意力层的有效性。对代表一大类多尺度纹理的多重分形纹理，在十次独立训练上进行定量评估表明，所提架构能够生成尺寸比原始纹理大 8×8 的纹理，且统计量准确。结果表明，将自注意力置于 critic（判别器）中可在不同输出尺寸下提供最一致的性能。

## Abstract
Multivariate scale-free texture images are nowadays central in numerous modern applications very different in nature. A common challenging issue consists of being able to generate such multivariate textures at any arbitrary size, from a learning set of (possibly much) smaller size. Parametric models often turn out to be too restrictive for the generation of realistic textures, thus prompting neural network training-based generation. While several neural network approaches propose generation at sizes larger than training, they do not explicitly assess the quality of the generation by quantifying how well multiscale or scale-free statistics in textures are reproduced and rather rely on perceptual assessment. The present work combines convolutional Wasserstein-GAN architectures with self-attention layers to generate textures of arbitrary size while focusing on reproducing multiscale statistics, without coding a model into the loss function. The relevance of using a self-attention layer is studied. Quantitative assessment conducted on multifractal textures, representative of a broad class of multiscale textures, over ten independent trainings shows that the proposed architecture is able to generate textures with accurate statistics for sizes $8 \times 8$ larger than the originals. The results show that placing self-attention in the critic provides the most consistent performance across output sizes.
