## 摘要
将旧视频重新搬上当代屏幕，并不必然使其看起来具有当代感。早期成像流程往往会留下色偏、色度黯淡、对比度不足和色调失衡等问题，而校正这些特征仍高度依赖专业调色师，并且难以扩展到长时、多镜头视频。我们提出长视频色彩现代化这一任务，即在保留场景内容以及跨帧、跨镜头一致性的同时，更新旧彩色影像的色彩与色调外观。我们引入一个可扩展、免训练的框架，利用预训练的文本引导图像编辑模型，对时间上相邻的关键帧网格进行联合现代化处理，在无需配对监督的情况下生成连贯的当代风格参考。随后，紧凑的镜头特定颜色变换将这些调整传播到每个镜头的全过程，同时保持空间结构和时间稳定性。我们在跨越五十年的多样化旧视频上进行了多维评估。我们将感知现代性的人工评估与内容保真度、时间稳定性、感知质量和效率的定量指标相结合，并引入源加权镜头间调色一致性（Source-Weighted Inter-shot Grade Consistency, SW-IGC）来评估跨镜头的调色一致性。在我们的评估集上，我们的框架取得了最高的感知现代性，同时在各项互补指标上保持竞争力，为长视频色彩现代化的系统研究开辟了道路。代码可在 https://github.com/jiwoomood/Video_Color_Modernization.git 获取。

## Abstract
Bringing legacy video back to contemporary screens does not necessarily make it look contemporary. Earlier imaging pipelines often leave color casts, muted chroma, weak con- trast, and tonal imbalance, while correcting these character- istics still relies heavily on expert colorists and scales poorly to long, multi-shot videos. We formulate long video color modernization, a task that updates the chromatic and tonal appearance of legacy color footage while preserving scene content and coherence across frames and shots. We introduce a scalable, training-free framework that jointly modernizes grids of temporally adjacent keyframes using a pretrained text-guided image editing model, producing coherent con- temporary references without paired supervision. Compact shot-specific color transforms then propagate these adjust- ments throughout each shot, preserving spatial structure and temporal stability. We conduct a multidimensional evaluation on diverse legacy videos spanning five decades. We combine a human evaluation of perceived modernness with quantita- tive measures of content fidelity, temporal stability, percep- tual quality, and efficiency, and introduce Source-Weighted Inter-shot Grade Consistency (SW-IGC) to assess grade co- herence across shots. On our evaluation set, our frame- work achieves the highest perceived modernness while re- maining competitive across complementary measures, open- ing a path toward the systematic study of long video color modernization.
