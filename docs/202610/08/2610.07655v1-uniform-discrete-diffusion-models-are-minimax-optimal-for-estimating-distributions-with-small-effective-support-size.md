## 摘要
离散扩散模型已成为在离散乘积空间上进行生成建模的一种实际成功的框架，但其统计泛化性质仍鲜为人知。离散真实世界数据（如文本或生物序列）由于语义或物理约束，往往集中在天文数字般巨大的环境空间的一小部分上，但现有界未能捕捉这种分布结构，反而随环境空间的大小而缩放，导致几乎空洞的误差界。我们针对均匀离散扩散填补这一空白，它是与掩蔽扩散并列的两种主流离散扩散范式之一，通过推导由有效支撑集规模 $s_n(P_0)$ 控制的统计保证，该量是依赖于样本量的分布复杂度度量。给定来自 $[K]^d$ 上未知数据分布 $P_0$ 的 $n$ 个独立同分布（i.i.d.）样本，我们证明，在适当选择网络规模和超参数时，期望总变差（TV）损失按 $O(\sqrt{s_n(P_0)/n})$ 缩放，而期望 Kullback--Leibler（KL）散度被 $O(\frac{1}{n}s_n(P_0)\log(eK^d/s_n(P_0))\log n)$ 界定。此外，我们证明 TV 速率是极小极大最优的，而 KL 速率在相差一个 $\log n$ 因子的意义下是极小极大最优的。综合来看，这些上界和下界表明，对于具有小有效支撑规模的分布，均匀离散扩散成功避免了维数灾难：TV 误差率仅通过 $s_n(P_0)$ 依赖于环境状态空间大小，而相应的 KL 速率仅额外对数依赖于环境状态空间大小。

## Abstract
Discrete diffusion models have emerged as a practically successful framework for generative modeling on discrete product spaces, yet their statistical generalization properties remain poorly understood. Discrete real-world data such as text or biological sequences often concentrate on a small fraction of the astronomically large ambient space because of semantic or physical constraints, but existing bounds fail to capture this distributional structure and instead scale with the size of the ambient space, giving rise to almost vacuous error bounds. We address this gap for uniform discrete diffusion, one of the two dominant discrete diffusion paradigms alongside masking diffusion, by deriving statistical guarantees governed by the effective support size $s_n(P_0)$, a sample-size-dependent measure of distributional complexity. Given $n$ independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) samples from an unknown data distribution $P_0$ on $[K]^d$, we show that, with appropriate choices of network size and hyperparameters, the expected total variation (TV) loss scales as $O(\sqrt{s_n(P_0)/n})$, while the expected Kullback--Leibler (KL) divergence is bounded by $O(\frac{1}{n}s_n(P_0)\log(eK^d/s_n(P_0))\log n)$. Furthermore, we show that the TV rate is minimax optimal and that the KL rate is minimax optimal up to a factor of $\log n$. Together, these upper and lower bounds show that uniform discrete diffusion successfully avoids the curse of dimensionality for distributions with small effective support size: the TV error rate depends on the ambient state-space size only through $s_n(P_0)$, while the corresponding KL rate incurs only an additional logarithmic dependence on the ambient state-space size.
