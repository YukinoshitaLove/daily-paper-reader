## 摘要
生成式重建能够从欠采样采集数据中恢复高质量图像，但当前先进的生成先验通常依赖迭代采样。减少迭代次数可降低推理成本，但也会失去渐进式分布细化的机会，从而难以在不牺牲重建保真度的情况下保持图像真实感。漂移建模提供了一种替代方案：将渐进式细化转移到训练阶段，从而无需迭代推理轨迹即可实现一步采样。然而，现有漂移方法在建模分布漂移时未考虑成像算子。我们提出 DriftRecon，一种用于一步式医学图像重建的生成方法，它直接在成像算子的零空间中执行漂移。DriftRecon 使用零空间受限噪声构建训练样本，并采用多尺度生成器在各分辨率层级融入测量条件，同时将其生成分量投影到算子零空间。为在推理时控制保真度与真实感之间的权衡，条件引导参数持续引导同一生成器在测量条件生成与无条件生成之间过渡。我们推导出一种依赖引导的相似核温度调度，该调度保留接受强化监督的引导加权质量，从而随着引导增强逐步将漂移交互局部化，以限制相关的重建保真度损失。在稀疏视角 CT 和加速 MRI 重建中的实验表明，DriftRecon 在单次前向传播中生成重建结果的同时，优于已有的生成式重建方法和近期的漂移模型。

## Abstract
Generative reconstruction can recover high-quality images from undersampled acquisitions, but state-of-the-art generative priors commonly rely on iterative sampling. Reducing the number of iterations lowers inference cost but also removes opportunities for progressive distributional refinement, making it difficult to retain image realism without sacrificing reconstruction fidelity. Drift modeling offers an alternative by shifting progressive refinement to training, thereby enabling one-step sampling without an iterative inference trajectory. Existing drifting methods, however, do not account for the imaging operator when modeling distributional drift. We propose DriftRecon, a generative method for one-step medical image reconstruction that performs drifting directly in the nullspace of the imaging operator. DriftRecon uses nullspace-restricted noise to construct training samples and a multiscale generator that incorporates measurement conditioning across resolution levels, with its generated component projected onto the operator nullspace. To control the fidelity--realism trade-off at inference, a conditional guidance parameter continuously steers the same generator between measurement-conditioned and unconditional generation. We derive a guidance-dependent similarity-kernel temperature schedule that preserves the guidance-weighted mass receiving strengthened supervision, thereby progressively localizing drift interactions as guidance increases to limit the associated loss of reconstruction fidelity. Experiments in sparse-view CT and accelerated MRI reconstruction show that DriftRecon outperforms established generative reconstruction methods and recent drifting models while producing reconstructions in a single forward pass.
