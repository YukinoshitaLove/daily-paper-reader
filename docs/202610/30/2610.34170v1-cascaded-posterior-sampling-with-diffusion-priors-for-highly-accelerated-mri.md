## 摘要
扩散模型是求解逆问题（包括加速MRI）的强大先验。然而，对高分辨率图像的分布进行建模需要大型网络，计算成本很高。我们提出一种后验采样框架，使用级联扩散模型作为多尺度先验。以粗尺度图像为条件时，细尺度细节的分布具有局部性且条件良好，因此可由小型网络捕获。类似地，小型无条件网络足以表示低分辨率图像，并用于从k空间中心恢复粗尺度图像。该估计随后作为高分辨率先验的条件，并与所有测量结合以恢复最终图像。两个先验均仅基于图像训练，因此适用于任意采样模式，并且数据一致性在每个尺度上保持线性。在多线圈脑部MRI中，所提方法在15倍加速下将PSNR相较于最佳单尺度扩散基线提高4–6 dB，而其每个单独训练的网络约小10倍。随着问题变得更加病态，增益增大；消融实验表明，该增益源于具有信息量的低分辨率条件。

## Abstract
Diffusion models are powerful priors for inverse problems, including accelerated MRI. However, modeling the distribution of high-resolution images requires large networks with high computational cost. We propose a posterior sampling framework that uses cascaded diffusion models as multiscale priors. Conditioned on a coarse image, the distribution of fine-scale detail is local and well conditioned, and it can therefore be captured by a small network. Similarly, a small unconditional network is sufficient to represent low-resolution images, and it is used to recover the coarse image from the k-space center. This estimate then conditions the high-resolution prior, which is combined with all measurements to recover the final image. Both priors are trained on images alone, so they apply to any sampling pattern, and data consistency remains linear at every scale. In multi-coil brain MRI, the proposed method improves on the best single-scale diffusion baseline by 4-6 dB in PSNR at 15x acceleration, while each of its separately trained networks is about 10x smaller. The gain grows as the problem becomes more ill-posed, and ablations show that it arises from informative low-resolution conditioning.
