## 摘要
表示对齐通过促使中间特征匹配预训练视觉编码器，来改进并加速扩散训练。然而，标准对齐在匹配预训练表示时，并未区分哪些信息可能对生成更有用。我们提出局部外观纯化（local appearance purification, LAP），它通过去除局部可预测内容并对齐到残差，从而提升生成质量，并使 MAE 等编码器成为更有效的对齐目标。LAP 仅修改对齐目标，无需改变扩散训练或推理。通过在固定编码器内进行受控干预，我们表明有效对齐取决于目标所强调的信息，而不仅仅是其包含多少图像信息。我们的理论给出了标准对齐的变分解释，并且在局部预测器固定时，给出以局部外观为条件的逐 token 信息界之和。为理解为何纯化对某些编码器的益处大于其他编码器，我们推导了一个精确的常数读出（constant-readout）检验，用于衡量在不使用扩散模型隐藏状态的情况下，目标能够被多好地匹配。LAP 在五种预训练表示（MAE、AIMv2、CLIP、DINOv2、DINOv3）上均提升了生成质量，其中对 MAE 的增益最大：在 ImageNet-256 上使用 SiT-B/2 时，它将 FID 从 $53.1$ 降至 $42.3$。其增益在更强的对齐方案和 tokenizer 下依然保持。在扩散训练之前计算的常数读出分数可以预测纯化带来的收益（在 13 个编码器上 Spearman $ρ=0.94$）。

## Abstract
Representation alignment improves and accelerates diffusion training by encouraging intermediate features to match a pretrained visual encoder. However, standard alignment matches pretrained representations without distinguishing what information may be more useful for generation. We introduce local appearance purification (LAP), which improves generation quality and makes encoders such as MAE more effective alignment targets by removing locally predictable content and aligning to the residual. LAP modifies only the alignment target, and requires no changes to diffusion training or inference. Through controlled interventions within a fixed encoder, we show that effective alignment depends on what information the target emphasizes, not simply how much image information it contains. Our theory gives a variational interpretation of standard alignment and, for fixed local predictors, a sum of token-wise information bounds conditioned on local appearance. To understand why purification benefits some encoders more than others, we derive an exact constant-readout test that measures how well the target can be matched without using the diffusion model's hidden state. LAP improves generation quality across five pretrained representations (MAE, AIMv2, CLIP, DINOv2, DINOv3), with the largest gain for MAE: on ImageNet-256 with SiT-B/2, it reduces FID from $53.1$ to $42.3$. Its gains persist with stronger alignment recipes and tokenizers. Computed before diffusion training, the constant-readout score predicts the benefit of purification (Spearman $ρ=0.94$ across 13 encoders).
