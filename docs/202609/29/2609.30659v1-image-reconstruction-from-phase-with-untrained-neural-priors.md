## 摘要
傅里叶相位编码了重要的图像空间结构，但在未测量频谱幅度的情况下恢复图像需要额外约束，并且会使绝对强度具有歧义。我们提出一种基于投影的两阶段框架，将傅里叶相位和空间支撑约束与特定图像的神经先验相结合。第一阶段交替执行约束施加与正则化神经先验更新，第二阶段仅进行相位/支撑细化，并保证收敛。我们在相同的77幅显微图像上评估了两种神经先验实现，并将其与仅使用约束的基线进行比较。在500次最终细化迭代后，表现最佳的变体达到31.41 dB的合并PSNR、35.75 dB的平均PSNR和0.9531的平均SSIM，相对于基线将合并PSNR提高了1.51~dB，并将合并MSE降低了29.3%。结果表明，在评估的迭代预算下，将神经引导与显式约束细化相结合具有优势，同时也表明仅较低的相位残差并不能保证更高的重建精度。

## Abstract
Fourier phase encodes important spatial image structure, but recovering an image without measured spectral magnitude requires additional constraints and leaves absolute intensity ambiguous. We propose a projection-based two-stage framework that combines Fourier-phase and spatial-support constraints with an image-specific neural prior. The first stage alternates constraint enforcement with regularized neural-prior updates, while the second performs phase/support refinement alone with guaranteed convergence. We evaluate two neural-prior implementations on the same 77 microscopy images and compare them with a constraint-only baseline. After 500 final refinement passes, the best-performing variant achieves 31.41 dB pooled PSNR, 35.75 dB mean PSNR, and 0.9531 mean SSIM, improving pooled PSNR by 1.51~dB and reducing pooled MSE by 29.3% relative to the baseline. The results demonstrate the benefit of combining neural guidance with explicit constraint refinement at the evaluated iteration budget, while showing that lower phase residual alone does not guarantee greater reconstruction accuracy.
