## 摘要
结构光照明显微镜（SIM）扩展了光学通带，而对其以外细节的重建依赖于先验知识。手工设计的正则化器取决于其结构假设与样本的匹配程度；学习得到的先验可能对成像条件和样本结构的变化敏感。我们提出 SIMAdapter，它首先在来自 BioSR、BioTISR 以及涵盖不同 PSF 和样本结构的模拟数据的 23,237 个原始 SIM 数据栈上进行自监督预训练，得到一个预测发射体和点扩散函数（PSF）的网络，然后将其适配到单个未标注的目标数据栈。适配过程利用可微图像形成模型对网络进行细化，其中光照模式由该数据栈校准。两个阶段均从原始测量中获取监督，无需配对的高分辨率参考。在两个留出的合成域上，SIMAdapter 达到的平均发射体归一化均方根误差为 0.156，而 Sparse-SIM 为 0.403。从仅在 BioSR 上预训练的网络开始进行相同适配，在两个域上精度均较低。在三项实验案例研究中，适配减少了侧翼伪影，并在丝状对、线粒体边界和校准线处得到更清晰的轮廓。因此，单个预训练网络可跨 SIM 测量重复使用，且每个重建结果都针对其自身的原始数据进行细化。

## Abstract
Structured illumination microscopy (SIM) extends the optical passband, and reconstruction of detail beyond it depends on prior knowledge. Hand-designed regularizers depend on how well their structural assumptions match the specimen; learned priors can be sensitive to changes in imaging conditions and specimen structure. We introduce SIMAdapter, which pretrains a network that predicts the emitter and the point-spread function (PSF) by self-supervision on 23,237 raw SIM stacks from BioSR, BioTISR, and simulations spanning different PSFs and specimen structures, then adapts it to a single unlabeled target stack. Adaptation refines the network against a differentiable image-formation model, with the light pattern calibrated from that stack. Both stages take their supervision from the raw measurements and need no paired high-resolution reference. On two held-out synthetic domains, SIMAdapter reaches a mean emitter normalized root-mean-square error of 0.156, compared with 0.403 for Sparse-SIM. The same adaptation started from a network pretrained on BioSR alone is less accurate in both domains. In three experimental case studies, adaptation reduces flanking artifacts and yields more distinct profiles across filament pairs, mitochondrial boundaries, and calibration lines. A single pretrained network can thus be reused across SIM measurements, with each reconstruction refined against its own raw data.
